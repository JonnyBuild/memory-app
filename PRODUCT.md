# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Style*Memory is designed for people building a smaller, more intentional wardrobe and for a client demo around Claire Germain's responsible fashion direction. The primary context is mobile: users review what they wore, prepare a silhouette, add a memory photo, or plan a small trip from a phone.

## Product Purpose

The app is a local-first wardrobe memory tool. It helps users compose silhouettes, remember where and when they wore them, prepare appointments, track reuse, and pack from a capsule wardrobe. Success means the experience feels like a real premium mobile product, not a throwaway demo.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, atelier-led. The product should feel like a private fashion archive: quiet, tactile, responsible, and elevated without becoming decorative.

## Anti-references

Avoid SaaS dashboard aesthetics, generic Tailwind card grids, playful icon-heavy menus, green eco-app cliches, fake luxury gold/black styling, and oversized rounded MVP components.

## Design Principles

- Let the garment carry the interface.
- Treat every screen like a page from a private wardrobe archive.
- Use restraint as a premium signal.
- Make memory photos feel real enough for a client demo.
- Keep the product mobile-first and task-focused.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast, visible focus states, and reduced-motion support. Keep all flows usable in French on 375px wide mobile screens. Generated demo imagery is decorative or contextual and must not be the only way to understand a control.
